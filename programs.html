The programs, in detail

Four programs of 8 to 12 weeks, built on flights you make locally and footage you record.

Program 01 · Stick control first

Smooth flying

Jerky footage comes from jerky hands. We drill slow, small inputs, combined moves like orbits and reveals, and a pre-flight routine — battery, props, compass, airspace, people — that becomes automatic before anything leaves the ground.

  • 10 weeks
  • Any drone
  • 2 flights a week
  • Open legal space

Program 02 · Before you launch

Airspace & Part 107

We work through airspace classes, sectional charts, where authorization is needed and how to request it, plus registration and remote ID. For those going commercial, a structured study plan for the Part 107 knowledge exam — we're not a testing center and we don't issue anything.

  • 10 weeks
  • Any pilot
  • 4 hours a week
  • Current FAA materials

Start this program

Program 03 · Footage that gets used

Aerial video

Beautiful aerials still bore people when they're four unedited minutes of drifting. We plan shot lists with purpose, set the camera properly for light, and cut sequences with pacing — plus a straightforward color workflow that doesn't require a film degree.

  • 10 weeks
  • Flying confidently
  • 4 hours a week
  • Editing software

Program 04 · Doing it properly

Paid work

Real estate, events, roof inspections and construction progress are the common first jobs. We cover quoting, scoping, delivering files clients can use, and the checks to make before accepting work — certification, insurance and permission to fly where the job is. We're coaches, not lawyers or insurers.

  • 8 weeks
  • Part 107 in progress
  • 4 hours a week
  • A portfolio piece
